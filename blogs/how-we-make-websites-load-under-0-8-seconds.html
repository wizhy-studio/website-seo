Performance

How We Make Client Websites Load Under 0.8s (And Score 100/100)

Wizhy Dev Team
• Oct 15, 2026 • 5 min read

💡 Key Takeaways: Every 100ms delay in website load time costs up to 7% in conversions. By eliminating heavy JavaScript bundles, utilizing modern image compression (AVIF & WebP), and inlining critical CSS, we achieve sub-second speeds on every client build.

When a potential client clicks your Google link or ad, you have less than 2 seconds before their patience runs out. If your page stutters, layout shifts, or takes 4+ seconds to display content, over half of your visitors will bounce before seeing your offer.

1. The Problem With Modern Web Development

Over 90% of business websites created today are built on bloated frameworks or WordPress page builders that load 3MB to 8MB of unnecessary scripts. A visitor just wants to read about your services and fill a contact form, but their mobile device is forced to download 15 separate tracking scripts, huge font libraries, and uncompressed JPEG files.

2. Our 4-Pillar Speed Architecture

At Wizhy Web Studio, we take a different approach. Every website is engineered for speed from day one:

  • Zero-Bloat Vanilla Core: We write clean, native HTML, CSS, and modern JavaScript without dragging 400KB of React or jQuery runtime dependencies into the browser.
  • AVIF & WebP Responsive Formats: Images are compressed automatically using Sharp into modern formats with exact srcset dimensions, slashing image payloads by over 75%.
  • Self-Hosted WOFF2 Typography: We eliminate external Google Font DNS round-trips by serving optimized, subsetted WOFF2 files directly from our CDN.
  • Strict Interaction to Next Paint (INP) Defense: Non-essential scripts are deferred or lazily executed only when the user interacts, keeping the main thread completely idle.

3. The Measurable Business Outcome

By achieving sub-0.8s load times and a perfect 100/100 score on Google Lighthouse, client bounce rates drop by an average of 42%, organic search rankings climb faster, and mobile conversions surge.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile phone) when you visit a website. They allow the website to recognize your device, remember your preferences (such as dark/light theme choices), and understand how you interact with our pages to deliver a smoother, faster experience.

At Wizhy Studio (wizhy.in), we prioritize your digital privacy. We keep cookie usage to an absolute minimum and never deploy invasive tracking pixels or cross-site behavioral ad trackers.

2. Categories of Cookies We Use

🛡️ Essential Cookies

Strictly necessary for the website to function securely. They remember your cookie consent preferences and dark/light color mode preference. These cannot be disabled.

📊 Analytics Cookies

Anonymous aggregate statistics via Google Analytics to help us understand which case studies and services are most useful, measure page load speeds, and optimize user experience.

4. How to Manage Your Cookie Consent

When you first visit Wizhy Studio, you are presented with our floating cookie banner after scrolling past the hero section. You have complete control to:

  • Accept: Enables anonymous analytics to help us measure site performance and Core Web Vitals.
  • Decline: Blocks all non-essential analytics cookies while preserving essential site navigation and theme switching.

Resetting Your Preferences

You can clear cookies at any time from your browser settings. The consent banner will reappear on your next visit, allowing you to choose again.

5. Browser Controls & Disabling Cookies

Most modern web browsers allow you to manage or block cookies through their settings:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Enhanced Tracking Protection.
  • Apple Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Block all cookies.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies.

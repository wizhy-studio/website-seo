Service Delivery & Fulfillment.

1. Digital Nature of Deliverables

Wizhy Web Studio (WWS) is a specialized web engineering, UI/UX design, and technical SEO studio. We do not sell or ship physical goods. All deliverables produced under our commercial agreements are 100% digital assets and electronic services.

2. Standard Project Timelines & Sprint Milestones

Project timelines commence immediately upon receipt of the initial project advance payment and required client assets (logos, content, access credentials). Standard delivery estimates:

🚀 Fast-Track Studio Websites

5 – 10 Business Days: Complete interactive Figma/code prototyping, responsive development, Core Web Vitals optimization, and live deployment.

🏬 Custom E-Commerce & Web Apps

10 – 21 Business Days: Bespoke product catalogs, checkout integrations, payment gateway configuration, and database architecture.

⚡ Technical SEO & Speed Audits

2 – 4 Business Days: Full diagnostic audit report, Core Web Vitals fixes, schema markups, and indexing roadmap.

📈 Monthly Retainer Deliverables

Continuous Monthly Sprints: Weekly performance monitoring, bi-weekly ranking updates, and monthly Core Web Vitals reports.

3. Digital Delivery Channels & Asset Handover

All digital products and source codes are transmitted via secure digital channels:

  • Source Code Repositories: Transferred via private GitHub or GitLab repository invitations upon final project settlement.
  • Design Systems & Wireframes: Interactive links and export packages provided via Figma.
  • Production Deployment: Directly configured and launched on client servers (Hostinger, AWS, Vercel, or custom VPS) with SSL certificate installation.
  • Documentation & Invoices: Delivered electronically as digitally signed PDF attachments via email.

4. Staging Review & Milestone Approvals

Prior to live release, all websites are deployed to a private, password-protected staging URL. The Client receives an interactive staging review window (typically 3–5 business days) to test all links, forms, and responsive viewports before final production migration.

5. Post-Launch 30-Day Warranty & Support Window

Every custom website project delivered by Wizhy Web Studio includes a complimentary 30-day technical warranty window covering bug fixes, browser compatibility patches, and Core Web Vitals maintenance to ensure flawless post-launch performance.
